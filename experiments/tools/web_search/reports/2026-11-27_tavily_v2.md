# Web search comparison: Tavily, settings v2

Date: 2026-11-27 · Tool: Tavily · Settings: `tavily_v2` · Queries: `queries.json` v1
Compared with: [v1 baseline](2026-11-27_tavily_v1.md) (re-scored with `egypt_sources.csv` +
`international_sources.csv`)
Status: **tested, not adopted**. The gain is too small for what it costs.

## Summary

v2 pushed Tavily toward a list of 105 citable sources (official, news, reports, listing and
booking sites) with `include_domains_mode: "prefer"`. Developer and broker pages dropped
(45% → 35%) and news rose a little (5% → 8%). But the share of results from the preferred list
barely moved (25% → 27%), official sources and reports stayed flat, social posts and unknown
sites went up, and results became less stable between runs. **"Prefer" only nudges the ranking;
it doesn't bring in the sources we want.**

## What changed from v1

One setting: `include_domains` = 105 domains from both source lists, with types official,
intl_official, news, business_news, intl_news, consultancy_report, data_aggregator,
listing_portal, travel_booking and str_data. `include_domains_mode: "prefer"`. Everything else
is the same as v1 (general, basic, country egypt, 10 results).

**Run:** 12 queries × 2 runs = 24 calls, 24 credits. 0 errors, 0 empty results.

## What got better

- **`sokhna_q6` (strategy fit)** is the clearest win: Hotelier Middle East, Realty Egypt,
  Egyptian Streets, presidency.eg and nat.gov.eg. That's 6 citable results per run.
- **`sokhna_q5` (supply)** now includes Trip.com and AirROI (short-stay occupancy data) instead
  of event-venue directories.
- **New citable sources appeared:** Amwal Al Ghad, Savills Egypt and Nawy's blog.
- **Fewer developer/broker pages** in 9 of 12 queries.

## Weaknesses

1. **"Prefer" barely changes which sources come back.** Only 27% of results are from the
   preferred list, against 25% in v1. The famous Arabic news sites (Youm7, Al-Masry Al-Youm,
   El Watan, Masrawy) and the official data sources (CAPMAS, IDSC, NUCA) still don't appear.
2. **More social posts.** `sokhna_q4` returned 4 Facebook posts per run, and `obour_q3` returned a
   Facebook video and a YouTube video.
3. **More unknown sites.** "Not in lists" rose from 14% to 22%: westcapital-eg.com,
   sadaninvestment.com.eg, flatandvilla.com, adorehotels.com subdomains and others. The source
   lists need a top-up.
4. **Less stable.** The two runs had different top 3 links in 5 of 12 queries. In v1, almost
   every query was stable.
5. **Slower.** About 2 s per call instead of 1 s, likely because of the 105-domain list.
6. **Still no dates and still paid-report pages** for `sokhna_q1`, same as v1.

## Why the famous sources may be missing

The queries are in English, but the big Egyptian news sites and many official pages are mostly
in Arabic. They may have no English page that matches. If so, no ranking setting will bring
them in. That needs to be tested, not assumed.
