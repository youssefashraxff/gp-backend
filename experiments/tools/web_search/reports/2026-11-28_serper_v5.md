# Web search comparison: Serper v5 (date filter per question group)

Date: 2026-11-28 · Tool: Serper · Compared with: Serper v2, v3, v4
Status: **tool decision closed: Serper with the 3-search design.** v3, v4 and v5 are within
run-to-run noise of each other, so the date-filter choice is made on principle, not on these
numbers.

## What changed

serper_v5 = serper_v3 site groups + the date filter (last 24 months) on the site-restricted
searches, **on** for market_division, area_projects, prices and demand_seasonality, **off** for
supply and strategy_fit. 1 run, 36 calls.

## Key finding: Google results change between runs

v5 should have matched v4 for four groups and v3 for two, because those settings are identical.
It didn't (demand 7.5 against 11.0; strategy fit 4.5 against 6.5). Comparing the URLs:

- For the **same query with the same settings**, runs about 15 minutes apart share only **0–5 of
  10 URLs** (e.g. `sokhna_q4` English sites: 0 shared; `sokhna_q5` open: 5 shared).
- In v1 and v2, the two runs were seconds apart and **identical**. That stability was most
  likely Serper returning a **cached** result for the repeated query, not Google being stable.
- `sokhna_q4` English sites came back **empty** in v5 but had 10 results in v3. Serper empties
  are rare but do happen.

**So differences of about ±3 relevant citable per group between single runs are noise.** The
differences between v3, v4 and v5 overall (12–14) are within that noise. The large differences
still hold: every Serper site-restricted version (12–14) beats Tavily v3 (5.6) and Serper v1
(4.4).

My earlier claim that "Serper results are stable, so 1 run is enough" was wrong, because it
rested on cached repeats.

## Decision

1. **Tool:** Serper.
2. **Design:** 3 searches per question: English open + Arabic site group + English site group,
   with site groups per question type (v3).
3. **Date filter:** on for market division, area projects, prices and demand; off for supply and
   strategy fit (v5). Chosen on principle: the evidence rule needs recent facts for prices and
   demand, while hotel counts and national plans stay valid for longer. The numbers here can't
   separate v3/v4/v5.
4. **Evidence rule:** every result keeps its date; undated or old results are flagged for the
   agent, not silently used.

## Notes for the build

- Results for the same query vary over time, so test runs must be recorded (A3's recorded
  responses) rather than compared live.
- Serper likely caches repeated queries for a short time, which helps cost during one agent run.
- To compare small design changes in future, use 3+ runs spread over time, not 1–2 runs in a row.
