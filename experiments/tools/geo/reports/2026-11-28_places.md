# Places: Google Places (New) vs Foursquare

Date: 2026-11-28 · Plan task: A3 · Cases: `places_v1` · Sites: `site_01` (Ali's Manara site, New Obour), `site_02` (El Obour City centre, control)

## Inputs and settings

- 6 kinds of business from Ali's M Hub concept: cafe, restaurant, pharmacy, grocery, sports/padel, mall.
- Radius 1 km and 3 km around the pin.
- **Google:** Nearby Search (New), place types per kind, ranked by distance, `maxResultCount` 20 (the API's maximum, no next page). Then Place Details for the 5 nearest places that have reviews, to see how fresh the reviews are.
- **Foursquare:** Places search (new API, service key, free Sandbox), text query per kind, up to 50 results.
- 47 Google and 36 Foursquare calls in total (site_01 was run twice because the first run hit network errors on the laptop). Every raw response is in `runs/places/`.

## Results

**Ali's site (site_01): almost nothing within 3 km**, and that is true. On the satellite map the pin is desert beside the Regional Ring Road, with farms across it.

| Within 3 km | Google | Foursquare |
|---|---|---|
| Places found (all 6 kinds) | 4: one cafe on the Ring Road, two groceries, a tile shop typed as "mall" | 1: a hospital, returned for "pharmacy" |
| Newest review of the places with reviews | 248–1,520 days old | none |

**Control site (site_02): built up, so the providers can be compared.**

| Per kind (6 kinds × 2 radii) | Google | Foursquare |
|---|---|---|
| Places found | 20 in 10 of 12 searches (the cap), 8 and 2 in the others | 0–22 (59 in total) |
| With a rating | 20–85 % per search | 0 (not returned on the free tier) |
| With opening hours | 10–55 % | 0 |
| Data last refreshed | not given | median 2–7 years ago |
| Wrong kind returned | "mall" brings textile shops and outlets | "pharmacy" brings a hospital |
| Errors | 0 | 0 |

- **Google's 20-place cap hides the 3 km results.** Because results are ranked by distance, for cafe, restaurant, grocery and mall the 3 km search returned **exactly the same 20 places** as the 1 km search. Foursquare's 3 km lists hold chains that Google's 20 never reached (Carrefour, Cook Door).
- **Matching the two lists failed:** only 1 place matched by name and distance, because Google mostly returns Arabic names and Foursquare English ones. The overlap number is not usable; the counts and fields above are.
- **Place Details returns at most 5 reviews**, the ones Google picks. On the control site the newest was 48–606 days old. That is enough to sample themes, not to count them.

## What it means

1. **Google is the clear choice.** Foursquare finds fewer places, gives no ratings, reviews or hours on the free tier, and its data is years old.
2. **One Nearby call is not "all places within the radius".** The tool must either split the area into smaller circles or use Text Search with pagination (up to 60 results), and it must record when a search hit the cap, so the agent never claims full coverage it did not get.
3. **Place types are loose.** The agent must check each place's `primaryType` and name, not trust the search type.
4. **Empty is a real answer at new-city sites.** For Ali's site the honest output is "4 places within 3 km", plus an escalation to the expert for the planned supply that maps do not show yet.

## Weak spots of this test

- One pass per site, so run-to-run variation was not measured.
- Correctness of each listed place was not checked on the ground; only counts, fields and obvious wrong kinds.
