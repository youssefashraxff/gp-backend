# Routes: Google Routes vs OpenRouteService vs OSRM

Date: 2026-11-28 · Plan task: A3 · Cases: `routes_v1` · Site: `site_01` (Ali's Manara site)

## Inputs and settings

- 5 places customers could come from: Cairo Airport, El Obour City, Heliopolis (Korba), Madinaty, 10th of Ramadan.
- Both directions (to the site and from it).
- **Google Routes:** traffic-aware, departures on the next Sunday (a workday) at 08:00, 13:00, 17:30 and 22:00, and Friday at 13:00, Cairo time. 50 calls.
- **OpenRouteService** (free key) and **OSRM** (free public server): no traffic, so one call per pair, on the points Google resolved for each place. 10 calls each.
- OpenRouteService's old address was shut down in September 2026 (410 Gone); the rerun used the new `api.heigit.org` address.

- **Time of day matters, moderately:** 8–27 % between the fastest and slowest departure. Friday 13:00 was the fastest in 8 of 10 directions; the slowest was Sunday 17:30 or 22:00.
- **The free tools often pick another road, and they are faster on paper.** Their distances are within 0.5 km of Google's in only 4 of 10 routes, and up to 4 km apart in the others (most for 10th of Ramadan). Their times are lower than Google's fastest time: OpenRouteService by 3–36 %, OSRM from 2 % higher to 29 % lower, because they assume empty roads.
- **Google's own "no traffic" time is not a free-flow time.** It was sometimes higher than its traffic-aware time, so only the traffic-aware times should be used.

## What it means

1. **Google Routes is the tool**: it is the only one that knows traffic, and the times customers actually face are what Site analysis needs.
2. **Compute each pair at a few fixed times** (e.g. Sunday morning peak, Sunday evening peak, Friday midday) and report the range, not one number.
3. **OSRM is a free fallback only when Google fails**, labelled clearly: no traffic, and possibly a different road.
4. **The site is 27–50 minutes by car from these 5 places**, so its catchment is drive-to, not walk-in: an input for Segments, not just Site analysis.

## Weak spots of this test

- Google's times are predictions for future departures, not measured trips.
- The 5 origins are our choice; the agent should take origins from the segments it is studying.
