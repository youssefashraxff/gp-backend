# Social: Serper with social-site filters

Date: 2026-11-28 · Plan task: A3 · Cases: `social_v1` · Area: New Obour

## Inputs and settings

- 6 questions about the area: cafes, padel, residents' groups and malls in Arabic; cafes and padel in English.
- Serper (Google Search), `gl: eg`. Each question asked 5 times: once with all 4 platforms in one filter `(site:facebook.com OR site:instagram.com OR site:x.com OR site:tiktok.com)`, and once per platform. 30 calls.
- No scraping: only what Google has indexed. Results are matched by title, because most links are Google redirects whose token changes on every call.

## Results

29 of 30 calls returned results (1 empty: X for the English padel question).

| Question | One combined call | 4 per-platform calls (unique results) | Found only by the per-platform calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cafes (Arabic) | 9 | 38 | 31 |
| Padel (Arabic) | 10 | 36 | 26 |
| Residents' groups (Arabic) | 10 | 40 | 34 |
| Malls (Arabic) | 10 | 40 | 30 |
| Cafes (English) | 9 | 36 | 28 |
| Padel (English) | 10 | 30 | 23 |

- **The combined call is capped at 10 results** and returns mostly Facebook. One call per platform finds 3–4 times as much, for 4 times the calls.
- **Arabic is the right language for this area.** 72 of 200 Arabic results name New Obour specifically (العبور الجديدة); only 4 of 87 English results do. English results drift to old Obour City or even New Cairo.
- **Arabic results have no dates.** Only 3 of 200 Arabic results carried a date, against 59 of 87 English ones. Freshness must be read from the post itself.
- **What it finds is useful:** New Obour residents' groups by district (Al Horreya, Al Karama, Al Magd), new mall launches and their sales ads, cafe and padel openings. This is demand and supply signal that maps do not have yet.
- **72 % of the links are Google redirects** (`google.com/goto?url=…`); the real post address has to be resolved before it can be cited.

## What it means

1. **Serper is enough for social search** (it was the web search choice already, same key, no new provider).
2. **Use Arabic queries, one call per platform** (Facebook and Instagram first; X returned the fewest results naming the place).
3. **Every post is undated and unverified evidence:** the agent may use it for themes and leads, never as a fact, and must resolve the redirect link before citing it.
4. This stays a "Could" feature in the spec. It is cheap to keep, but it is not a replacement for the expert.

## Weak spots of this test

- "Names the place" is a keyword check; it does not prove the post is about the site's catchment.
- Google indexes only a small, public part of Facebook groups.
