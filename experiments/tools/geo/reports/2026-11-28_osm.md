# OSM: public Overpass servers vs a local copy of the data

Date: 2026-11-28 · Plan task: A3 · Cases: `osm_v1`, `osm_v2`, `osm_v3` · Sites: `site_01`, `site_02`

## Inputs and settings

The same 8 questions in every version: what the pin is inside; roads within 1 km; major roads within 3 km (nearest one: distance, type, name); land use within 1 km; buildings within 500 m and 1 km; points of interest within 1 km and 3 km. OSM is the only source for roads, land use and buildings: Google sells businesses, routes and elevation, not this map data.

| Version | Where the answers come from |
|---|---|
| v1 | 2 public Overpass servers (`overpass-api.de`, `kumi.systems`), default Python client |
| v2 | 3 public servers (+ `private.coffee`), descriptive User-Agent and Referer, 5 s between calls |
| v3 | **Local:** Geofabrik's Egypt extract (data of 2026-11-27 21:21 UTC) read once into a small cache of east Greater Cairo by `osm_data.py`, then the same questions answered on the laptop |

## Results

| Version | Calls | Answered | Time per answer | Why the rest failed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 | 32 | **0** | n/a | `overpass-api.de`: 406, then "connection refused" (it rejects the default client, then blocks the IP); kumi: timeouts |
| v2 | 48 | **7** (5 with data, 2 empty) | 31–42 s | 502 / 504 from the main server; no answer in 90 s from the other two |
| v3 | 16 | **16** | under 2 s (first call loads the cache) | none |

**v3 gives the same data as Overpass.** Where both answered, the numbers match exactly: control-site land use 81 = 81, points of interest 11 = 11 and 72 = 72, roads at Ali's site 27 = 27.

## What it means

1. **Public Overpass is not usable for the agent.** 7 of 80 calls answered across v1 and v2, most of them after more than 30 seconds. Every failure would become a needless escalation.
2. **OSM data is usable from a local copy.** v3 answered everything, fast, with the data's date as evidence.
3. **Use OSM for roads and access** (nearest major road, road types and names): it was right on both sites.
4. **Do not use OSM for buildings, or for land use in new cities.** Both are missing or thin around Obour. The agent must say "not mapped" and escalate, never read it as "empty land".
5. **Jurisdiction is not reliable from OSM** in new cities; keep it as an expert question.

## Weak spots of this test

- Two sites, both in the Obour area. Older districts of Cairo are mapped better.
- The local copy is a day old at build time; production needs a refresh schedule (Geofabrik publishes daily).
